## Long description (4000 char limit)

```
SyncTogether turns any link into a shared room. Watch YouTube videos in perfect
sync, build a group music queue, run a focused study session, or share photos and
videos straight from your phone — all with the people you invite, and all free.

No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No paywalled features.

WATCH TOGETHER — YOUTUBE IN SYNC
Paste a link or search YouTube inside the app and everyone in the room sees the
same frame at the same time. Play, pause and seek stay synchronized across every
device, with automatic drift correction so nobody falls behind. Theatre mode and
fullscreen give you a clean viewing surface, and the room ID is always one tap
away when you want to invite a friend mid-session.

MUSIC TOGETHER — A SHARED QUEUE
Turn the room into a listening party. Search songs, add them to a collaborative
queue and let everyone see what's playing and what's next. A compact mini player
keeps previous, next, play and pause controls within reach, so the host can steer
the session without reconnecting.

STUDY & RESEARCH ROOMS
Study together without leaving the app. The built-in research browser lets you
search and open web pages inside the room, share a link with everyone in one tap,
and keep collaborative notes and tasks alongside what you're reading. A shared
focus timer keeps the whole group on the same Pomodoro cycle.

SHARE FROM THIS DEVICE
Send photos, videos and audio from your own device directly to the room over an
encrypted peer-to-peer connection. Your files are never uploaded to our servers
and disappear when the session ends. Queue up to 50 photos or 20 videos and let
viewers browse at their own pace, or keep everyone on the same slide.

CHAT, REACTIONS AND SAFETY
Every room has persistent chat with reactions, editing and deletion. Report
content or people in a tap, block anyone you don't want to hear from, and rely on
a real moderation team behind the report queue. Hosts can transfer the room,
remove members and set ephemeral rooms that expire on their own.

PRIVACY YOU CONTROL
Separate consent switches for analytics, personalization and ads. Choose a room
data retention window. Export your data or delete your account and everything
tied to it from inside the app — no email required.

WHY SYNCTOGETHER
• Watch YouTube together in real time with friends, family or classmates
• Collaborative music queue everyone can add to
• In-app research browser with shared notes, tasks and a group focus timer
• Peer-to-peer local media sharing that never touches a server
• Room chat, reactions, reporting, blocking and host controls
• Invite by link or room code in seconds
• Completely free — supported by ads, with no purchases anywhere

Whether it's a long-distance movie night, a revision session before exams, a
playlist swap or a research sprint with your team, SyncTogether keeps everyone on
the same page — literally.

Create a room. Share the code. Start together.

SyncTogether uses the official YouTube embedded player and follows YouTube's terms;
background and audio-only playback of YouTube content is not available.
```

Character count is under the 4000 limit with room for localisation.
